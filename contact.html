Get in Touch

If you need help with the site, want to report a bug, have a privacy question, or are a rights holder with a concern, the fastest way to reach the project is by email.

Email: youxijidi@gmail.com

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Site feedback: suggestions to improve the homepage, game pages, guides, or user experience
  • Bug reports: loading issues, control problems, audio glitches, or browser compatibility bugs
  • Privacy requests: questions related to analytics, cookies, or data handling
  • Rights-holder concerns: trademark, copyright, or attribution questions
  • General inquiries: questions about the project, modes, or available content

How to Write a Helpful Message

To help us understand your request quickly, please include as much of the following information as possible:

  • The page URL where the problem happened
  • Your browser and device type
  • A short description of what you expected to happen
  • What actually happened instead
  • Screenshots, if they help explain the issue

For policy, privacy, or rights-related messages, please clearly identify the nature of the request in the email subject line.

Response Expectations

General Support

We aim to review general questions, bug reports, and feedback within a reasonable timeframe. Because this is a small independently maintained project, response times may vary.

Privacy and Legal Requests

Requests related to privacy, personal information, copyright, or trademark concerns are prioritized when possible. If you are contacting us in an official capacity, please include your full name, organization, and the basis for your request.

Project Note

Pac-Man Arcade is presented as an unofficial fan and educational project experience. The website is designed to make classic arcade-style gameplay accessible in the browser and to help players better understand the mechanics of maze chase games through guides, modes, and learning features.

If you are trying to reach the official Pac-Man rights holder or an official Bandai Namco support team, please use their official channels rather than this project email.
